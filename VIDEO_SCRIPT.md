# Praja Raksha: demo video script (about 3 minutes)

## Before recording
- Record in the early morning or late night, when Gemini is fastest.
- Do one full practice run first (it also "warms up" the AI so the real run is quicker).
- Use Chrome, zoom 110%, close other tabs, hide bookmarks bar. Never show the `.env` file or API key.
- Keep `samples/whatsapp_threat.png` ready to upload.
- Fill your details in the complaint form (name, a valid 10-digit phone, address, Mysuru) so the Filing Agent can finish.
- Tool: Windows **Clipchamp** (built in, records screen + mic and lets you cut). Or OBS Studio.
- If an AI step takes long, cut the wait in editing and add a small label "sped up". Never fake a result.

## Script

| Time | Screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Home page hero | "Meet Priya, a college student in Mysuru. Her ex-boyfriend is threatening to leak her private photos unless she pays fifty thousand rupees. She doesn't know which law protects her, she's scared of the police station, and she only feels comfortable in Kannada. Millions of Indians are in the same position. This is Praja Raksha, your AI legal shield." |
| 0:20–0:35 | Scroll: live news "Near you", helplines | "The home page shows live crime news for your city and one-tap helplines. But the real power is the complaint flow." |
| 0:35–1:10 | File complaint → choose ಕನ್ನಡ → tap mic and speak (or click the Kannada example) → upload the WhatsApp screenshot → Get legal help | "Priya just speaks in Kannada and uploads the chat screenshot. Now six AI agents start working. Watch the panel on the right." |
| 1:10–1:35 | Results: severity, law cards, Kannada summary, press **Listen** | "The Evidence Agent read the screenshot: the accused's name, phone and UPI ID, with a timeline. The Legal Agent found extortion, criminal intimidation and stalking under the new BNS, and explains it in Kannada, even out loud. It can only cite laws from a verified database, so it cannot invent sections." |
| 1:35–2:10 | FIR draft → tick consent → File my complaint → agent's browser filling the portal → acknowledgement number | "The Drafting Agent wrote her FIR complaint as a PDF. Now the Filing Agent opens the complaint portal in a real browser, reads each page, fills it, fixes any errors, and brings back the acknowledgement number, all on its own. For safety we use a demo portal that works like the government one." |
| 2:10–2:30 | Track this complaint → Demo: "15 days pass" → SP letter appears | "If police don't act within the 14-day legal window, the Tracking Agent escalates automatically and writes her letter to the Superintendent of Police under BNSS 173(4)." |
| 2:30–2:45 | Police & lawyers map: Cyber Crime Police Station ranked first, reviews, free legal aid | "Prefer to go in person? Praja Raksha shows the nearest police stations, the cyber crime station first for her case, free legal aid, and lawyers, with citizen reviews checked by our Moderation Agent." |
| 2:45–3:00 | Back to the home hero | "Praja Raksha: speak your problem, know your rights, get it filed. Six AI agents, three languages, built so that no victim has to give up. Thank you." |
